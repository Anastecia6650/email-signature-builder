How To Install Your Signature

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer.
  2. Click the Settings gear.
  3. Click See all settings.
  4. Stay on the General tab.
  5. Find the Signature section.
  6. Create a new signature.
  7. Click inside the signature editor.
  8. Click Copy Signature above and paste it into Gmail.
  9. Choose whether to use it for new emails and replies.
  10. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Tip: Send yourself a test email after installing it.

Microsoft Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Find Email signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the signature editor.
  7. Copy the signature above and paste it.
  8. Choose whether to use it for new messages and replies.
  9. Save your changes.

Tip: Outlook web and desktop can have slightly different menus.

Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail on your Mac.
  2. Click Mail → Settings.
  3. Open the Signatures tab.
  4. Select your email account.
  5. Click the + button.
  6. Paste your copied signature.
  7. Select the signature for your account.

Tip: Send yourself a test email.
