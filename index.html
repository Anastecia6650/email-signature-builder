How to install your signature

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer.
  2. Click Settings → See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab.
  4. Find the Signature section.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the signature editor.
  7. Click COPY SIGNATURE on this page.
  8. Paste the signature into Gmail.
  9. Choose when Gmail should automatically use it.
  10. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Tip: Send yourself a test email.

Microsoft Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Find Email signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the signature editor.
  7. Click COPY SIGNATURE on this page.
  8. Paste the signature.
  9. Choose whether to use it for new messages and replies.
  10. Save your changes.

Tip: Outlook web and desktop can have different menus.

Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail on your Mac.
  2. Click Mail → Settings.
  3. Open Signatures.
  4. Select your email account.
  5. Click + to create a signature.
  6. Click COPY SIGNATURE on this page.
  7. Paste it into the signature area.
  8. Select the signature for your account.

Tip: Send yourself a test email.
