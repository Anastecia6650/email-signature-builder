How to install your signature

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer.
  2. Go to Settings → See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab.
  4. Find Signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the signature box and paste your copied signature.
  7. Under Signature defaults, select it for New emails and Reply/forward.
  8. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
Send yourself a test email to check the signature.

Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Find Email signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the editor and paste your signature.
  7. Choose whether to use it for new messages and replies.
  8. Save your changes.
Send yourself a test email after saving.

Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail on your Mac.
  2. Click Mail → Settings.
  3. Open Signatures.
  4. Select your email account.
  5. Click + to create a signature.
  6. Paste your copied signature.
  7. Select the signature for the account.
Send yourself a test email to make sure everything looks correct.
