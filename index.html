How To Install

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer.
  2. Click Settings → See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab.
  4. Find Signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the signature editor.
  7. Paste your copied signature.
  8. Choose when Gmail should automatically use it.
  9. Click Save Changes.

Tip: Send yourself a test email.

Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Find Email signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Paste your copied signature.
  7. Choose whether to use it for new emails and replies.
  8. Save.

Tip: Outlook web and desktop can look different.

Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail on your Mac.
  2. Click Mail → Settings.
  3. Open Signatures.
  4. Select your email account.
  5. Click +.
  6. Paste your copied signature.
  7. Select the signature for the account.

Tip: Send yourself a test email.
