How to install your signature

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer.
  2. Click Settings → See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab.
  4. Find the Signature section.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the signature editor.
  7. Paste your copied signature.
  8. Choose whether Gmail should automatically use the signature for new emails and replies.
  9. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Tip: Send yourself a test email.

Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Find Email signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the editor.
  7. Paste your copied signature.
  8. Choose whether to automatically use it for new emails and replies.
  9. Save.

Tip: Outlook web and desktop can have slightly different menus.

Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail on your Mac.
  2. Click Mail → Settings.
  3. Open Signatures.
  4. Select your email account.
  5. Click + to create a signature.
  6. Paste your copied signature.
  7. Select the signature for the appropriate account.

Tip: Send yourself a test email after installation.
