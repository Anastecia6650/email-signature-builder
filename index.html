How to Install Your Signature

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer.
  2. Click the Settings gear.
  3. Click See all settings.
  4. Stay on the General tab.
  5. Scroll down to Signature.
  6. Create a new signature.
  7. Click inside the signature editor.
  8. Click Copy Signature above, then paste it into Gmail.
  9. Choose when Gmail should automatically use the signature.
  10. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Tip: Send yourself a test email afterwards.

Microsoft Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Find the Email signature section.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the signature editor.
  7. Paste your copied signature.
  8. Select whether to automatically add it to new emails and replies.
  9. Save your changes.

Tip: Outlook web and desktop can have slightly different menus.

Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail on your Mac.
  2. Click Mail → Settings.
  3. Select Signatures.
  4. Select the email account.
  5. Click the + button.
  6. Give the signature a name.
  7. Click inside the signature area.
  8. Paste your copied signature.
  9. Select the signature for the appropriate email account.

Tip: Send yourself a test email after installation.
