How to install your signature

Choose your email platform.

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer.
  2. Click the Settings gear.
  3. Click See all settings.
  4. Stay on the General tab.
  5. Scroll down to Signature.
  6. Create a new signature.
  7. Click inside the signature editor.
  8. Paste the copied signature.
  9. Select when Gmail should automatically use it.
  10. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
Tip: Send yourself a test email after installing it.

Outlook

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply.
  4. Find Email signature.
  5. Create a new signature.
  6. Click inside the editor.
  7. Paste the copied signature.
  8. Select whether it should be used for new emails and replies.
  9. Save your changes.
Tip: Outlook web and desktop may have slightly different menus.

Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail on your Mac.
  2. Click Mail → Settings.
  3. Open the Signatures tab.
  4. Select your email account.
  5. Click the + button.
  6. Paste your copied signature.
  7. Select the signature for that account.
Tip: Send yourself a test email to check the result.
